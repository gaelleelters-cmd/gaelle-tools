Privacy policy

How this site handles cookies, analytics, and your data. Last updated: 4 August 2026.

Who runs this site

This website (gaelleelters.com) is operated by Gaelle El Ters. Contact: info@gaelleelters.com.

What this site does

Gaelleelters.com is a small hub of practical browser tools (for example WhatsApp helpers, Mail Mass, and document generators). Most tools run in your browser. No account is required to browse the site or use the tools, unless a specific feature asks you to sign in (for example Mail Mass with your own Microsoft Outlook mailbox).

Local file processing

When you upload or open a file in a tool, processing normally stays on your device. Files are not uploaded to a server unless a tool clearly asks you to authenticate with a third party (such as Microsoft for sending mail from your own mailbox). Help-bot questions you choose to send may be delivered by email so they can be answered.

Cookies and similar technologies

This site uses a small set of cookies and storage:

  • Essential / functional — needed for security and basic delivery. That includes cookies or scripts managed by the hosting edge (Cloudflare), such as bot-protection, email-address obfuscation, and network error reporting. These are used to keep the site available and secure.
  • Preference — your cookie choice is stored in localStorage on this domain so the banner does not reappear on every visit. That preference is essential to remembering your decision.
  • Analytics (optional) — non-essential measurement is only enabled if you choose Accept. If Cloudflare Web Analytics or a similar beacon is enabled at the CDN layer outside this site’s HTML, that traffic may still be collected by Cloudflare; this site’s consent banner still records your preference for any site-controlled analytics scripts.

Your choices

On first visit you can choose Accept (allow optional analytics if present) or Essential only (decline non-essential analytics). You can change your mind anytime via in the footer, or reopen the banner from the home page.

Third parties

  • Cloudflare — CDN, TLS, and security features for gaelleelters.com.
  • Google Fonts — may receive your IP when fonts are loaded.
  • Microsoft — only if you sign in through Mail Mass to send from your mailbox.
